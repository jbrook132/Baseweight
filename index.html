Why it exists

I got sick of doing this on a spreadsheet.

I'm Jack. I'm a physiotherapist, I hike a lot, and I make videos about the gear that actually works. Which means I spend an unreasonable amount of my life weighing things.

The problem was never the weighing. It was everything after. Every tool for tracking a kit list was built for a desktop browser in an era before anyone thought you'd want this on a phone — and a phone is exactly where you want it. In the tent, the night before, at the gear shop working out whether that pack is worth the swap.

So I built the thing I wanted. It's free, it runs on your phone and your laptop, and it does one job properly.

What it does

The whole job, nothing else.

No social feed, no badges, no premium tier hiding the useful bits.

  • Catalogue 500+ pieces of real gear, weights loadedStart typing "x-mid" and it fills the weight in for you. Tents, quilts, pads, packs, stoves, filters, clothing — men's and women's cuts both.
  • Locker Your own gear, saved onceWeigh your kit properly, put it in the locker, and it's there for every list you ever build. One tap to add.
  • Base weight Worn and consumable, handledTag your boots as worn and your food as consumable. Base weight, pack weight and total sort themselves out.
  • Breakdown See where the weight actually isCategory split, heaviest items ranked, and your pack as a percentage of your bodyweight.
  • Checklist Pack without forgetting thingsFlip any list into a checklist the night before and tick it off as it goes in the pack.
  • Import Bring your kit over in one fileAlready have a list somewhere else? Export it as a CSV, import it here, and your whole kit lands with its categories, quantities and worn or consumable tags intact.
  • Share Export as an image or a CSVPost your kit list or breakdown as a clean image, or take the numbers elsewhere as a spreadsheet.
How to use it

Four steps. About ten minutes.

The first list is the only one that takes any effort. After that your gear's already saved.

Start a list

One per trip, or one per setup — summer, winter, bikepacking, whatever you run. Name it and go.

Add your gear

Type the name and pick it from the catalogue with the weight already filled in, or add your own and put it on the scales. Already got a list elsewhere? Import the CSV and skip this step entirely.

Tag what's worn or eaten

Boots and trekking poles are worn. Food, fuel and water are consumable. Tag them and your base weight is calculated properly.

Check the breakdown, then pack

See what's heavy, decide what stays home, then run the checklist the night before and load the pack.

What's different

Built for the place you actually use it.

The breakdown does the thinking. Most tools give you a list and a total, which tells you that your pack is heavy but not why. Baseweight ranks your categories, shows your heaviest items, and puts your pack next to your bodyweight — so you stop guessing and start cutting the right things.

It's a phone app that respects your laptop. Every other gear list tool is a website you pinch and zoom while standing in a cold car park. This one is designed for the small screen first and opens up properly on a big one.

The weights are already there. Over 500 items with manufacturer specs loaded, tents listed at minimum trail weight, and clothing labelled with the cut and size it was weighed in — so you know exactly what you're adjusting from.

Switching takes one file. Export a CSV from whatever you're using now, import it here, and your entire kit arrives with its categories and tags already set. You're not retyping forty items to try something new.

It works with no signal. Once it's loaded it keeps working in the tent, on the plane, halfway up a hill in Scotland.

It's free. Not free-for-now, not free-with-three-lists. Free.
